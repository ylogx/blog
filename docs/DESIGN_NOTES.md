# Design Notes

## Image Philosophy
- **Blogs are text-first**: Images don't convey as much meaning in blog posts as they do in other content types
- **Minimize image prominence**: Large hero images on the homepage are unnecessary and take away from the content
- **Focus on typography and content**: The design should emphasize readability and content hierarchy over visual imagery
- **Images as accents**: When images are used, they should be smaller, supporting elements rather than dominant features

## Design Decisions
1. Homepage hero section should be text-focused with minimal or no large images
2. Post previews can have small thumbnails but shouldn't be image-heavy
3. Single post pages should prioritize content over large cover images
4. Typography and spacing are more important than visual imagery
